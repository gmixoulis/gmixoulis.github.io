The survey, as text

This browser couldn't draw the 3D map, so here is everything on it as plain text.

I'm George Michoulis. I live in Thessaloniki, Greece, and since May 2026 I've worked remotely as a Web3 full-stack developer at Cyberscope by TAC. Before that I built blockchain systems at Sidroco Holdings and the University of Nicosia, lectured for the University of Derby at Mediterranean College, and published research on verifying academic qualifications with blockchains.

The benchmark: now

Web3 full-stack developer at Cyberscope by TAC, since 05/2026: end-to-end Web3 products, contracts, on-chain integrations, React and Next frontends, and security tooling.

Email is the quickest way to reach me: gmixoulis@gmail.com. I'm also on LinkedIn, GitHub, Google Scholar and X.

Ridges: where I've worked
Sidroco Holdings Ltd, blockchain developer and researcher, 2024 – 2026
An NFT marketplace for 5G, Hyperledger Fabric prototypes, an Erasmus+ learning management system, and multinational EU projects.
University of Derby / Mediterranean College, lecturer and academic partner, 2023 – 2025
I taught Networks & Security, Web Scripting and Application Development, with inclusivity-first assessment.
University of Nicosia / IFF, blockchain and full-stack developer, 2022 – 2024
Large Next.js, NestJS and Docker applications, and a Web3 NFT marketplace.
Rivers: what I studied
MSc Data and Web Science, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, 2020 – 2022
Graph-embedding methods for blockchain fraud detection, funded by a DeepMind scholarship.
BSc Applied Informatics, University of Macedonia, 2015 – 2020
My thesis was on verifying academic credentials with Ethereum.
Peaks: what I've published

Exploring decentralized governance: a framework applied to Compound Finance. Intl. Conf. on Mathematical Research for Blockchain Economy, 2023. Cited 7 times.
