# Round-4 brief: immersive WebGL without the predictable patterns

George: "The immersive WebGL was perfect, but the patterns were really predictable and AI-looking."
See `round3-patterns.png` in this folder for what NOT to produce.

## Banned patterns (in any disguise)
1. The standard hero: giant name + one-line subtitle + `[Contact →] [See work]` button pair.
2. The vertical section stack: a scene in the background with HTML sections headed About / Experience /
   Papers / Certificates / Contact scrolling over it.
3. Dates-left experience rows: `2024 – 2026 | Title / Org in accent colour / blurb`.
4. The citation-number list: huge numerals + title + venue.
5. The certificate thumbnail grid + "See all 34".
6. The contact finale: a giant email + a `LinkedIn · GitHub · X · Scholar` row.
7. The generic thin top nav: `About Work Papers Proof Contact`.
8. The Research / Build / Teach triad.
9. The stock intro line: "I'm a blockchain developer and researcher. I build smart contracts, graph-based ML
   and full-stack Web3 systems."
10. Demo-famous WebGL tricks as the main idea: particles forming text, a morphing glass blob, iridescent
    cubes, a fluid sim background, golden-hour water, an infinite tunnel, an isometric room, a floating
    low-poly island.

## Instead
- **The 3D world IS the interface.** Content lives inside the scene, and the information architecture
  comes from the concept.
- **One primary interaction model**, obvious within 3 seconds, with a tiny elegant hint.
- The name "George Michoulis" is present and legible at the start (in the world or as a corner mark).
- **Always reachable:** a small persistent corner with the email, and a "Read as text" toggle showing ALL
  the content as a plain accessible page (also the no-WebGL path).
- **The camera** moves only on user-initiated scroll, click, drag or keys, smooth and damped, and never
  follows the pointer.
- Microcopy is short, specific and human. Facts only.
