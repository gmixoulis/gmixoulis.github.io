A garden is not a gallery. Nothing here is finished, and nothing has to be.

This is where I keep notes that do not fit a paper or a repository: half-formed
thoughts on graphs and consensus, things I read, places I walked, code I wrote
at the wrong hour and liked anyway.

## Raked sand

The stones stay. The sand gets raked again every morning. I like that as a
model for writing in public: the ideas that matter are heavy enough to remain,
and the rest is allowed to be redrawn.

> Sit quietly, doing nothing, spring comes, and the grass grows by itself.

Expect short entries. Expect gaps. The gaps are part of it.
