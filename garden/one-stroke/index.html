One stroke, no corrections

What the ensō taught me about shipping.

  • #craft

An ensō is drawn in a single breath. You do not go back over the thin part. The dry gaps where the brush ran out of ink are the point.

Most of my worst code came from going back over the thin part.

// leave the gap
export const draw = (brush: Brush) => brush.stroke({ passes: 1 });

The circle is closed or it is not. Either way it is done.
