About

George Michoulis is a software engineer based in Thessaloniki, Greece, moving into agentic software engineering. He builds full-stack and Web3 products and has published research on blockchain systems. BSc Applied Informatics (University of Macedonia) on Ethereum credential verification. MSc Data and Web Science (Aristotle University) on graph embeddings for drug-target interaction prediction, funded by DeepMind. Currently Web3 Full-Stack Developer at Cyberscope by TAC; previously Blockchain Developer & Researcher at Sidroco Holdings LTD and Lecturer at University of Derby / Mediterranean College. Available for blockchain, Web3, and research collaborations — Thessaloniki and remote.
