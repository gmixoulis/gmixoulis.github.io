To a researcher,

The longest thread in my research is verifying academic qualifications with a blockchain. It began as my BSc thesis at the University of Macedonia.

In 2020 I introduced VerDe, a verification system built on Ethereum, at the XIV Balkan Conference on Operational Research. That paper has 11 citations. Two years later a process-aware approach to the same problem appeared in Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory. With 32 citations, it is my most cited paper.

I’ve also written about decentralized governance, with a framework applied to Compound Finance (7 citations), and about blockchain marketplaces for 5G network slicing, including FraMark, which uses fractional NFTs.

My MSc in Data and Web Science at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki was on graph-embedding methods for predicting drug-target interactions. A DeepMind scholarship funded the degree.

If your work overlaps with any of this and you need a co-author, or someone who can build the prototype, email me at gmixoulis@gmail.com.

Best wishes,
