The years 2015 to 2026, as an orrery

Degrees and jobs ride on arms that extend only during their real years. Moons are papers, sized by citations, and the stones set in the rings are awards. Click one to read its plaque.

Drag the handle round, or scroll. Each turn is one year.

Write to me

gmixoulis@gmail.com

I live in Thessaloniki and work remotely, writing smart contracts and researching ledgers with graph-based machine learning.

2015 to 2020

I studied for a BSc in Applied Informatics at the University of Macedonia. My thesis was on verifying academic credentials with Ethereum. In 2020 I introduced VerDe, a verification system built on that idea, at the XIV Balkan Conference on Operational Research (Verification of Academic Qualifications through Ethereum Blockchain: An Introduction to VerDe, 11 citations). Two Greek-language theses on the same topic followed, in 2020 and 2021, and the university gave me a Basic Research grant for 2020–2021.

2020 to 2022

A DeepMind scholarship paid for my MSc in Data and Web Science at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, where I worked on graph-embedding methods for predicting drug-target interactions. In June 2022 I took third place at the Infinitech Hackathon, run by Crowdpolicy.

Three papers came out in 2022. A process-aware approach for blockchain-based verification of academic qualifications (Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory 121, 102642) is my most cited, with 32 citations. Graph Embedding and Node Features for Drug-Target Interaction Prediction is from my MSc work at AUTH. Blockchain in Higher Education: permissioned and permissionless approaches appeared at the 4th Summit on Gender Equality in Computing.

2022 to 2025

At the University of Nicosia’s IFF I built Next.js, NestJS and Docker apps and a Web3 NFT marketplace. In 2023 I published Exploring decentralized governance: a framework applied to Compound Finance at the International Conference on Mathematical Research for Blockchain Economy (7 citations).

From 2023 to 2025 I lectured for the University of Derby at Mediterranean College, teaching Networks & Security, Web Scripting and Application Development. I also give public talks on Web3 and edge tech.

2024 to 2026

At Sidroco Holdings I built an NFT marketplace for 5G, Hyperledger Fabric prototypes and an Erasmus+ LMS. Two papers from these years deal with 5G marketplaces: FraMark: A Blockchain Marketplace for 5G Network Management using Fractional NFTs (7th World Symposium on Communication Engineering, 2024, 1 citation) and Unlocking 5G network slicing: a survey on blockchain marketplaces utilizing NFTs, AI, and advanced resource management (IEEE ICCE 2025). Data security for smart cities also came out in 2025 (1 citation), the same year the Move/Sui Bootcamp in Thessaloniki gave me an award.

Since May 2026

I’m a Web3 full-stack developer at Cyberscope by TAC. I build Web3 products end to end: contracts, on-chain integrations, React and Next frontends, and security tooling.
