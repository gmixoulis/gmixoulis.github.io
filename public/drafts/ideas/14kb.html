George Michoulis

Blockchain developer and researcher. I work on smart contracts, graph-based machine learning, and full-stack Web3 systems.

Since May 2026 I have been a Web3 full-stack developer at Cyberscope by TAC. I live in Thessaloniki, Greece, and work remotely. The quickest way to reach me is gmixoulis@gmail.com.

Background

I studied Applied Informatics at the University of Macedonia and wrote my thesis on verifying academic credentials with Ethereum. For my MSc in Data and Web Science at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, funded by a DeepMind scholarship, I worked on graph-embedding methods for detecting fraud on blockchains.

The work has three sides. I research how effective ledgers are and what they change in practice, in papers and in startup work. I build: NFTs and DeFi on the Web3 side, graph embeddings on the data side, shipped in Solidity, TypeScript and the cloud. And I teach: I have lectured on networks, security and application development, and I give talks on Web3 and edge technology.
