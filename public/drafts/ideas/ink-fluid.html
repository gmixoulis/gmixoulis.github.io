About

I'm George, a blockchain developer and researcher based in Thessaloniki. I write smart contracts, study graph-based machine learning, and build Web3 systems from the contract to the frontend.

I studied Applied Informatics at the University of Macedonia, where my thesis verified academic credentials on Ethereum. My MSc in Data and Web Science at the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, funded by a DeepMind scholarship, looked at graph-embedding methods for predicting drug–target interactions.

Research
Publications and startup work on how effective ledgers are, and what they change in practice across blockchain systems.
Build
Web3 products (NFTs, DeFi), data science with graph embeddings, shipped in Solidity, TypeScript and the cloud.
Teach
Lecturing in networks, security and application development, and speaking in public about Web3 and edge tech.
