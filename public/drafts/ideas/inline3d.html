I’m a blockchain developer and researcher in Thessaloniki, and this is an account of my work since 2015.

The small objects set into the text are drawn live. Hover over one, or tap it, and a note opens under its line.

I

Proof of a degree

Two degrees in Thessaloniki, and a thesis on proving you hold one.

I studied Applied Informatics at the University of Macedonia from 2015 to 2020. My BSc thesis was about verifying academic credentials on Ethereum. In 2020 I introduced VerDe, a system for verifying qualifications on Ethereum, at the Balkan Conference on Operational Research, and in 2020–2021 the university gave me a Basic Research grant .

The MSc in Data and Web Science at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki came next, from 2020 to 2022, funded by a DeepMind scholarship . I worked on graph-embedding methods for drug–target interaction prediction, which also became a paper.

II

Thirty-two, eleven, seven

Eight papers, and how often each one is cited.

Most of my citations go to one paper , a process-aware approach to verifying academic qualifications on a blockchain, published in Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory in 2022. It follows my 2020 introduction to VerDe at the Balkan Conference on Operational Research. In 2023 I turned to governance, with a framework for decentralized governance applied to Compound Finance . The 5G work came next. FraMark , presented at the 7th World Symposium on Communication Engineering in 2024, is a marketplace for 5G network management built on fractional NFTs, and a survey of blockchain marketplaces for network slicing followed at IEEE ICCE 2025.

There are three more papers. One covers data security for smart cities (2025), one compares permissioned and permissionless blockchains in higher education (2022), and the third is the drug–target interaction work from my MSc . Two Greek-language theses on verifying academic titles with Ethereum, from 2020 and 2021, and startup work on how effective ledgers are in practice fill in the rest. The full list is on Google Scholar.

III

Four jobs since 2022

Product teams, EU research and a lecture hall, with the dates overlapping.

The University of Nicosia and IFF hired me in 2022 as a blockchain and full-stack developer. Until 2024 I built Next.js, NestJS and Docker applications there, along with a Web3 NFT marketplace. In June 2022 I also took third place at Crowdpolicy’s Infinitech Hackathon .

From 2023 to 2025 I was a lecturer and academic partner with the University of Derby and Mediterranean College , teaching Networks & Security, Web Scripting and Application Development. I also give public talks on Web3 and edge tech.

At Sidroco Holdings , from 2024 to 2026, I worked as a blockchain developer and researcher on an NFT marketplace for 5G, prototypes on Hyperledger Fabric, an Erasmus+ learning management system and EU projects. In 2025 I received an award at the Move/Sui Bootcamp in Thessaloniki .

Since May 2026 I’ve been a Web3 full-stack developer at Cyberscope by TAC . I build Web3 products end to end, from the contracts and on-chain integrations to the React and Next frontends and the security tooling.

IV

Things with a hostname

Most of what I’ve built for other people lives on a university domain.

What I build runs on Solidity, TypeScript and the cloud, with NFT and DeFi products on one side and data science with graph embeddings on the other. Some of it has a public address. VerDe has its own site at verde.uom.gr. For the University of Nicosia I built the metaverse course site and the sign-in page of its Accelerate platform . The Blockchain Benchmarking Framework has a landing page that names the University of Nicosia, Ripple and XRP Ledger as sponsors.

Closer to home I built two sites at Aristotle University, a Greek-language one for the Centre for European Legal Culture and one for the EUDEM Jean Monnet Chair . At the University of Macedonia I built the site of MarLab , its Marketing Laboratory.

V

A folder of thirty-four

Degrees, courses, workshops and a few prizes. Each title opens its scan.

Besides the two degrees, a BSc in Applied Informatics and an MSc in Data and Web Science, the folder holds the Move bootcamp award and a school progress award from the Greek Ministry of Education. Most of the rest are courses: a CCNA course, Rust development, machine learning and deep neural networks, cloud engineering, advanced ethical hacking, networking essentials, software development for telecommunication networks, a cybersecurity e-learning academy, how to design and build a website, how to get published with IEEE, an introduction to digital currencies, the NFT Talents program, the Hour of Code, entrepreneurship and the funding of new enterprises, digital skills for tourism, PepsiCo School’s Discover Customer, the European Legal Culture program at Aristotle University and a Greek e-learning course on marketing and branding. The workshops and talks cover a RippleX workshop and launch event, EY Cyber Day, the GNU/Linux command line, VeChain ToolChain in healthcare, data analytics for healthcare cybersecurity, blockchain for the legal industry…, blockchain in education, remote learning and social distancing…, processing data in the fog on the RAINBOW platform, a real-estate chatbot project at SKGCode and the Hydrobot program in marine educational robotics. Two are about English: a TOEIC listening and reading test and the Michigan certificate of proficiency in English.
