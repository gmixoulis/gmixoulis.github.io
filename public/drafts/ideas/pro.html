About

I build blockchain systems and study how well they work in practice.

I'm based in Thessaloniki. My BSc in Applied Informatics at the University of Macedonia ended with a thesis on verifying academic credentials on Ethereum. For my MSc in Data and Web Science at Aristotle University, funded by a DeepMind scholarship, I worked on graph-embedding methods for blockchain fraud detection.
