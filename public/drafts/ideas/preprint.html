Abstract

I am a blockchain developer and researcher based in Thessaloniki. I write smart contracts and full-stack Web3 systems, I use graph-based machine learning, and I have taught networks, security and application development. The work began with a BSc thesis on verifying academic credentials on Ethereum, which became the VerDe system and a 2022 paper that is now my most cited, with 32 citations. An MSc at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, funded by a DeepMind scholarship, moved me to graph-embedding methods for blockchain fraud detection. Since May 2026 I build end-to-end Web3 products at Cyberscope by TAC.

Keywords: smart contracts; academic credentials; graph embeddings; 5G marketplaces; DeFi governance; teaching

1Introduction

I build and study blockchain systems. Most days that means smart contracts and the full-stack software around them. On the research side it means asking how effective a ledger really is, and what it changes in practice once it is running.The page is set as a preprint: numbered sections, figures, a reference list. Every claim in it can be checked against the references or the tables.

It started with my BSc in Applied Informatics at the University of Macedonia, where my thesis verified academic credentials on Ethereum.That thesis became the VerDe paper [2] and, two years later, the process-aware approach [1]. The top row of Figure 1. I then took the MSc in Data and Web Science at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki on a DeepMind scholarship, working on graph-embedding methods for blockchain fraud detection.Graph embeddings also went into a drug–target interaction study [7].

The work has three sides. (i) Research: publications and startup work on how effective ledgers are and what they change in practice, from academic credentials to 5G marketplaces and DeFi governance. (ii) Building: Web3 products with NFTs and DeFi, data science with graph embeddings, shipped across Solidity, TypeScript and the cloud. (iii) Teaching: I have lectured on networks and security, web scripting and application development, and I speak in public about Web3 and edge technology. Figure 1 shows how the pieces connect.
