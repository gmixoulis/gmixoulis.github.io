Ideas for the new site

Rough prototypes built from real content and existing assets. Round 6 is the current direction: the cube and particle look from round 3, in six new layouts.

Round 6: the cube and the particles, new layouts

  1. 1

    Callouts

    The cube stays centred and your facts attach to it with leader lines, like a technical drawing.

  2. 2

    Type as window

    One giant word per chapter, with the particles visible only through the letters.

  3. 3

    One screen

    No scrolling. A numbered index reconfigures the cube and particles, and content swaps into fixed zones.

  4. 4

    3D inside the text

    A live cube is the "o" in George, and tiny 3D figures sit inside the sentences.

  5. 5

    Orbit ring

    Content panels circle the cube; scroll turns the ring to bring the next one to the front.

  6. 6

    Depth sandwich

    Particles behind, giant type in the middle, and the glass cube in front, bending the words.

Round 5: a physical process you operate

  1. 1

    Sand table

    A steel ball draws each chapter into raked sand. Pick a word on the rim.

  2. 2

    Orrery

    A brass machine of your decade. Turn the crank and roles orbit through their real years.

  3. 3

    Noren

    Part cloth curtains with your hand to walk into each room.

  4. 4

    Darkroom

    Drop blank sheets into the developer and watch your record appear under the safelight.

  5. 5

    Loom

    Work the loom and your decade weaves itself into cloth, row by row.

Round 4: WebGL, the world is the interface

  1. 1

    Powers of Ten

    One continuous zoom: Earth, Thessaloniki, a desk, a screen, code, a hash, a bit.

  2. 2

    Torchlight

    A dark archive you explore with a beam of light. Pull objects forward to read them.

  3. 3

    Gravity

    Scroll is time. Each year's work falls in with real physics and piles into your CV.

  4. 4

    The film

    No scrolling: a real-time film of the decade that you scrub like a video.

  5. 5

    Topography

    Your career as terrain built from real data: papers are peaks, roles are ridges.

Round 3: immersive WebGL

  1. 1

    Ink in water

    A real-time fluid simulation: sumi ink blooms and swirls as you scroll and stir.

  2. 2

    Glass sculpture

    One refractive glass object that morphs section by section: ensō, pebble, blocks, crystal.

  3. 3

    Particles

    100k particles form your name, an ensō, and a nebula of your real papers.

  4. 4

    Thessaloniki at dusk

    A cinematic flight along the waterfront, from golden hour to night, as you scroll.

  5. 5

    Product-grade

    No concept, just flagship polish: an iridescent 3D block and precise components.

Round 1: printed-object concepts

  1. 1

    大福帳 Daifukuchō: the ledger book

    An Edo merchant's account book. Each entry gets a red hanko seal as it scrolls in.

  2. 2

    The Preprint

    The site typeset as a research paper. Figure 1 is a graph of your real work.

  3. 3

    The Exhibition

    Your work as museum objects with wall labels, plus a floor plan as the navigation.

  4. 4

    Graph-native

    A typographic graph of your papers, roles and degrees, used as the map of the page.

  5. 5

    絵巻 Emaki

    One ink handscroll read top to bottom, with scenes beside sumi paintings.

  6. 6

    GM-101 syllabus

    Your career as a course you teach, with red-pen marks on the key facts.

  7. 7

    The Profile

    A long-read magazine feature, told in your voice.

Round 2: breaking the frame

Round 1 turned the site into a printed object read top to bottom. These don't. Each one breaks one of those assumptions.

  1. A

    The Verifier

    Drop one of your certificates onto the page and the browser checks its fingerprint against the published list. Your research, working.

  2. B

    One sentence

    The whole homepage is one sentence. Click a phrase to unfold the detail in place.

  3. C

    Who's asking?

    Hiring, researching or studying: the same facts, reordered for each reader.

  4. D + I

    Card on Thessaloniki time

    A business card whose light follows your clock in Thessaloniki, not the visitor's.

  5. E

    Three formats

    One CV as page, text or JSON, all rendered from a single source.

  6. F

    Thessaloniki map

    Your career drawn on a map of your real city, not a borrowed costume.

  7. G

    The 14 KB site

    Everything in one network round trip. No images, fonts or scripts.

  8. H

    Letters

    Three short letters in your voice: to someone hiring, a researcher, a student.

  9. J

    Ledger Run as the front door

    The playable résumé first, with an equal-weight "skip to the plain CV".

Drafts only. Nothing here is wired into the live site. The current UI is saved on the astro-rebuild branch.
