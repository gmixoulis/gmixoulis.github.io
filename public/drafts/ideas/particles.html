About

I’m based in Thessaloniki. My BSc thesis at the University of Macedonia verified academic credentials on Ethereum. My MSc at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, funded by a DeepMind scholarship, looked at graph-embedding methods for predicting drug-target interactions.
