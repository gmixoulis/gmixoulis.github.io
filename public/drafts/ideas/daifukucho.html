大福帳序一丁

Preface

I’m George, a blockchain developer and researcher based in Thessaloniki, Greece. I work remotely.

I studied Applied Informatics at the University of Macedonia, where my thesis verified academic credentials on Ethereum. Then came an MSc in Data and Web Science at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, funded by a DeepMind scholarship, where I worked on graph-embedding methods for predicting drug-target interactions.

The work has three sides, and I keep an account for each.

  • 研Research

    Publications and startup work on how effective ledgers are, and what they change in practice across blockchain systems.

  • 作Build

    Web3 work on NFTs and DeFi, data science with graph embeddings, and shipping across Solidity, TypeScript and the cloud.

  • 教Teach

    Lecturing in networks, security and application development, and speaking in public on Web3 and edge tech.

証

Edo-period merchants kept their accounts in a daifukuchō, a thick hand-bound ledger, and confirmed entries with a red seal. I build ledgers and have written papers on verifying credentials, so this book is kept the same way. Each entry gets the seal 証, “proof”.
