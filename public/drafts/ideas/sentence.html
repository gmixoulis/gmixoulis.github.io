, and I build smart contracts,

Right now I’m a Web3 full-stack developer at Cyberscope by TAC (since May 2026). I work there end to end: contracts, on-chain integrations, React and Next frontends, and security tooling.

From 2024 to 2026 I was a blockchain developer and researcher at Sidroco Holdings: an NFT marketplace for 5G, Hyperledger Fabric prototypes, an Erasmus+ learning management system, and multinational EU projects. From 2022 to 2024 I built large Next.js, NestJS and Docker apps, and a Web3 NFT marketplace, at the University of Nicosia and IFF.

Most days that means Solidity, TypeScript and the cloud. Along the way I also built seven sites for universities and research groups.

research graph embeddings,

My MSc in Data and Web Science at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (2020–2022) was funded by a DeepMind scholarship. The research was on graph-embedding methods for drug–target interaction prediction, which also became a paper.

Since then I’ve kept doing research and startup work on how effective ledgers really are and what they change in practice, from a governance framework tested on Compound Finance to NFT marketplaces for 5G networks.

Before all of it came a BSc in Applied Informatics at the University of Macedonia (2015–2020), and a few prizes along the way.

teach networks and security,

From 2023 to 2025 I was a lecturer and academic partner for the University of Derby at Mediterranean College. I taught Networks and Security, Web Scripting, and Application Development, and I wrote the assessments with inclusivity first.

Outside the classroom I give public talks on Web3 and edge tech.

and write about verifying degrees on Ethereum

It started as my BSc thesis on checking academic credentials with Ethereum and became VerDe (verde.uom.gr). These are the papers about it:

  • A process-aware approach for blockchain-based verification of academic qualifications. Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory 121, 102642, 2022. 32 citations
  • Verification of Academic Qualifications through Ethereum Blockchain: An Introduction to VerDe. XIV Balkan Conference on Operational Research, 2020. 11 citations
  • Blockchain in Higher Education: permissioned and permissionless approaches. 4th Summit on Gender Equality in Computing, 2022. no citations yet

Plus two Greek-language theses on the same question, in 2020 and 2021. The rest is about governance, 5G networks and data: everything else I’ve published.
