Where it starts

I studied Applied Informatics at the University of Macedonia and wrote my thesis on checking academic credentials with Ethereum. That work led to VerDe and a run of papers on blockchains in higher education.

For my MSc in Data and Web Science at Aristotle University, funded by a DeepMind scholarship, I worked on graph embeddings for blockchain fraud detection. That is why this page opens on a graph. I live in Thessaloniki, Greece, and work remotely.

Research. My papers and startup work ask whether a ledger changes how something works in practice: credential checks, governance in decentralised finance, and marketplaces for 5G network resources built on NFTs.

Build. I ship Web3 products end to end: smart contracts in Solidity, TypeScript front ends, cloud deployments, Hyperledger Fabric prototypes, and data work with graph embeddings.

Teach. I have lectured on networks and security, web scripting and application development, and I give public talks on Web3 and edge tech. In teaching I set assessment with inclusivity first.
