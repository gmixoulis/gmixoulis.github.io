1Course description

I’m George, a blockchain developer and researcher based in Thessaloniki. I work remotely, mostly on smart contracts, graph-based machine learning and full-stack Web3 systems. This course is the record of how I got here and what I can show for it.

I studied Applied Informatics at the University of Macedonia, where my thesis asked how to verify academic credentials on Ethereum. I then took an MSc in Data and Web Science at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, funded by a DeepMind scholarship, and worked on graph-embedding methods for drug–target interaction prediction.

The work has three sides. I research: publications and startup work on how effective ledgers are, and what they change in practice across blockchain systems. I build: Web3 products such as NFT marketplaces and DeFi, data science with graph embeddings, shipped across Solidity, TypeScript and the cloud. I teach: I have lectured in networks, security and application development, and I speak in public on Web3 and edge tech.

2Learning outcomes

By the end of the course you should have seen enough evidence to agree that I can:

  1. Design, write and ship Solidity smart contracts and the on-chain integrations around them, from NFT marketplaces to DeFi, with security tooling alongside.
  2. Build full-stack Web3 products in TypeScript: React and Next.js frontends, NestJS services, Docker, and the cloud underneath.
  3. Apply graph-embedding methods, as in my MSc work on drug–target interaction prediction, and to other graphs.
  4. Prototype permissioned ledgers on Hyperledger Fabric, and design marketplaces around fractional NFTs, as with FraMark for 5G network management.
  5. Take a research question through to a working system, as with VerDe, which verifies academic qualifications on the Ethereum blockchain.
  6. Teach networks and security, web scripting and application development with inclusivity-first assessment, and speak in public on Web3 and edge tech.
