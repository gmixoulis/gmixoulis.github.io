On the wall

I'm a developer and researcher in Thessaloniki, Greece, and I work remotely. Most of what's kept here is about blockchains: the products and smart contracts I build, and papers on verifying academic credentials, on governing a DeFi protocol, and on a marketplace for 5G network resources built on fractional NFTs.

My MSc, funded by a DeepMind scholarship, used graph embeddings to look for fraud on blockchains. I've also lectured in networks, security and application development, and I give talks on Web3 and edge tech.
