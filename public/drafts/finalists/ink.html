About

George Michoulis is a blockchain developer and researcher based in Thessaloniki, Greece, working on smart contracts, graph-based machine learning and full-stack Web3 systems.

I work remotely. I build Web3 products (NFTs, DeFi) and data-science tools with graph embeddings, using Solidity, TypeScript and the cloud. I studied Applied Informatics at the University of Macedonia, where my thesis verified academic credentials on Ethereum. My MSc in Data and Web Science at the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, funded by a DeepMind scholarship, ended with a thesis on graph embeddings for drug-target interaction prediction.
